### [00:00 – 00:15] The Problem
"Every year, millions of students and engineers look at job postings and wonder: *'Why am I not getting callbacks?'*  
Candidates know their own skills, and job postings list requirements. The painful blindspot is in between: understanding exactly what technical gaps you have, why they matter, and what to build to fix them. But uploading your complete employment history, contact details, and resume to public cloud AI tools surrenders your private career data to corporate cloud servers."

### [00:15 – 00:35] The Solution
"That’s why we built **CareerEdge AI** — private, on-device career intelligence engineered specifically for Snapdragon PCs.  
CareerEdge AI analyzes your resume, evaluates a target job description, and computes an explainable, deterministic alignment score. But instead of giving you a generic percentage, it answers the decisive question: *'Why is my profile not aligned?'* It isolates your top critical skill gaps, explains the missing evidence, and synthesizes a personalized 30-day roadmap with daily milestones and portfolio capstone blueprints."

"Best of all, the entire AI pipeline runs 100% locally.  
Using ONNX Runtime and the Qualcomm QNN Execution Provider, CareerEdge AI leverages the Snapdragon Hexagon NPU for semantic embeddings in just 4 to 6 milliseconds. That’s more than twelve times faster than host CPU inference, with virtually zero impact on your laptop's battery life."

### [00:55 – 01:15] Privacy & Sovereignty
"Turn off your Wi-Fi, go into airplane mode, and CareerEdge AI continues to run seamlessly. Your resume never leaves your Snapdragon PC. Zero cloud API calls, zero telemetry, and zero subscription costs. When you're finished, one click permanently wipes all local session data."
